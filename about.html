After studying Design and 3D in Rome, I moved to London to pursue my dream of working in video games. That decision opened the door to an industry where creativity, technology, and interaction constantly meet. Over time, I found my strongest focus in UX/UI design:

the bridge between people and the systems they interact with, from games to apps and digital products. It is the area where I can combine visual design, problem-solving, and empathy to create experiences that feel clear, intuitive, and engaging.
